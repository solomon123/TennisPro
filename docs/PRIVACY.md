# TennisReplay privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-10_

TennisReplay records tennis matches on your phone, lets you keep score from a Wear
OS watch, and analyses the recordings on your phone to find serves, estimate their
speed and call the service line. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: **everything stays on your devices. The app has no
account, no analytics, no ads, and does not send your recordings anywhere.**

## What the app handles

| Information | Why | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera video and microphone audio | Recording a match you start | Saved only in the app's own storage on your phone |
| Court calibration (four corner points and a reference frame) | Measuring serves and line calls | Saved only on your phone |
| Serve results (times, speeds, line calls) | Showing them in Replay | Saved only on your phone, next to the recording |
| Match score, watch taps and alerts | Scoring from the watch | Sent between your own phone and watch — see below |

Serve detection, including body-pose detection, runs entirely on your phone. No
image, video or audio is uploaded for processing.

## Phone and watch

The phone and watch talk to each other through Google Play services' Wearable Data
Layer. Only the match score, your taps, and short alerts (such as a line call) are
sent — never video or audio. These messages normally travel over Bluetooth or your
local network; when the devices are not directly connected, Google Play services
may relay them through Google's servers. That relay is operated by Google under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). TennisReplay's
developer does not receive these messages.

## Permissions

- **Camera** and **Microphone**: to record the match you start. Recording shows a
  persistent notification for as long as it runs.
- **Notifications**: to show that recording, or serve analysis, is in progress.

## Sharing, retention and deletion

TennisReplay does not collect, sell or share personal information with the
developer or any third party. Recordings stay until you delete them — one at a
time or several at once from the app — or uninstall the app, which removes all of
its recordings, calibration and results. Android's cloud backup is switched off for
this app, so none of it is copied to your Google account.

## Children

TennisReplay is not directed at children under 13.
